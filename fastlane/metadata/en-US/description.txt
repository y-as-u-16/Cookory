Cookory is a simple app for keeping a photo record of the meals you cook.

■ Recording takes one tap
No dish name required, no rating required. The moment you pick a photo, the record is saved. Add the dish name and notes later, when you have a moment. The app is built around "record now, organize later."

■ Your cookbook grows on its own
Recorded dishes gather into a cookbook automatically. Cook karaage twelve times and it stays one entry — with "Cooked 12 times" next to it. Only the dishes you actually made appear.

■ See a dish get better over time
Open a dish and its cooking history appears in order.

2026/08/29 Rated 5 - More potato starch made it crispier
2026/08/12 Rated 4 - A little too salty
2026/07/15 Rated 3 - Tried double frying

Knowing how last time went helps the next time.

■ A calendar made of your photos
Your meal photos fill a monthly calendar. Tap a date to see what you cooked that day.

■ Remember the dishes you have not made in a while
The home screen surfaces dishes you have not cooked recently, so good ones do not quietly disappear from the rotation.

■ Search by dish name or note
Search covers both dish names and the notes you wrote.

■ Everything stays on your device
Photos and notes are never transmitted anywhere. The app performs no network communication, works fully offline, and requires no account.

■ Take your data with you
Export every record as a ZIP from Settings. Photos and JSON are included, so you can keep your data before switching devices or deleting the app.

A cooking log you can actually keep up with.
